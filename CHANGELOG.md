# Changelog

## 1.0.0 (not yet released)

The first version for friends.

- **Dances to Claude's spinner word** while Claude works, travelling across his
  strip: each kind of word has its own dance, every other word gets a mix of
  its own, and only "Moonwalking…" gets the moonwalk.
- **Reacts to what Claude does**: peers while it reads, hammers while it edits,
  types while it runs commands, a victory dance or a facepalm at test results,
  confetti on a git commit, a bow when a reply is done. He never changes or
  slows anything Claude does.
- **Calm between turns**, and **naps** (`z Z`) after 3 quiet minutes, using no
  power until your next message wakes him.
- **Toys**: hover over him for ♥, ☕ and ♪, or press 1 2 3 on his strip.
- **`/clawd`**: the dance card, 22 moves to collect, two of them secret.
- **`/clawd-chill`**: he stops dancing and chills. Remembered between sessions.
- **Day mode**: on a light theme he won't dance; he's a nerd in glasses and a
  bow tie who types while Claude works.
- **`/clawd-demo`**: every move in a row.
- Drawn so no frame shows a hairline in terminals with odd-width cells.
- Easter eggs. We're not saying.
